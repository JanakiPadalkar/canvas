# Food-Tech Data Science

## About This Repository

This repository documents my learning journey in Data Science for food delivery and digital marketplaces, with a focus on **Statistics, Machine Learning, and Operations Research**.

My goal is to understand how data-driven systems solve practical business and operational problems, from predicting demand to allocating delivery partners efficiently.

## Learning Focus

- **Statistics & Experimentation:** Hypothesis testing, A/B testing, and causal inference.
- **Demand Forecasting:** Understanding demand patterns and predicting future order volumes.
- **Machine Learning:** Recommendation systems, ranking, and delivery-time prediction.
- **Operations Research:** Assignment optimisation, routing, scheduling, and resource allocation.
- **Business Decision-Making:** Translating model outputs into operational decisions.

## Learning Roadmap

## My Approach

For each topic, I aim to understand the business problem, identify the relevant statistical or mathematical methods, implement the approach where appropriate, and interpret what the results mean for decision-making.

This repository will evolve as I learn, experiment, and build projects.
